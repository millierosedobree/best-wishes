Best Wishes, takes its name from its first group exhibition: a presentation of drawings sent and collected over 2021, installed across the desks of an office on Camberwell New Road vacated during the pandemic. The space was occupied shortly after by a gathering of office desk mascots made by invited participants.

Since then, Best Wishes, has organised exhibitions in repurposed office spaces across London and Amsterdam, including Amsterdam’s former District Court, held by @bologna.cc, and at 1 Hoxton Street. In April 2025, it took over the Plaster Magazine store as an alternative format for curating and selling work.

Best Wishes, has grown into an expanded practice of curation as co-making, where the roles of artist, gallerist, and friend overlap in a spirit of collaboration. Next Door is its first permanent space: a former bike shed adjacent to Millie Rose Dobree’s home in East London. From here—and often extending into the street or the flat itself—Best Wishes, runs a programme of solo and small group exhibitions that lend themselves to this intimate space.
